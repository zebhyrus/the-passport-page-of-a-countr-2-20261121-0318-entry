An open passport spread for the invented tide-bound Republic of Velloria, capital Aldhaven. Holder Maren Solvig Alden, lightkeeper at North Light, issued 12 Mar 2023. The opposite page carries arrival and departure stamps from Velloria's three neighbours only — Drenmark, Solvarre and Kessaly — all dated after issue, ending with her return to Aldhaven on 12 May 2023. The telling detail is the Tide Permit: the Aldhaven Causeway may be crossed only at low tide, within two hours either side of low water, fee 4 korun paid.
